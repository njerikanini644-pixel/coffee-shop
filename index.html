Our story

We opened with one espresso machine, a handful of stools and a simple idea: coffee should taste like where it came from, not like how long it sat on a shelf.

We buy directly from small farms, roast in small batches and bake our pastries every morning. No rush, no gimmicks.

What we care about

Fresh beans. Roasted weekly, ground when you order.

Fair sourcing. We pay farmers above market price.

A seat for everyone. Free Wi-Fi, plugs at every table and a quiet corner for reading.
